\section{Architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

The top-level system is shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}. A \SI{35}{\giga\hertz}
reference is delivered via wirebond, buffered, and split into two quadrature
paths by a reflection-based $90^\circ$ coupler. The direct (in-phase) output
of the coupler provides the $x$-phase input to the current-mode phase
interpolator (CMPI), while the coupler output passes through a transmission
line to introduce the orthogonal $y$-phase. The CMPI produces an interpolated
phase $\phi_\mathrm{PI}$ at \SI{35}{\giga\hertz}, which is then doubled to
\SI{70}{\giga\hertz} by a $\times 2$ frequency multiplier. To suppress
free-running noise beyond the locking bandwidth, a direct \SI{35}{\giga\hertz}
injection path locks the doubler to the reference.
