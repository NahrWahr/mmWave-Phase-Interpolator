\begin{abstract}
  This paper presents the design and analysis of a millimeter-wave (mm-wave)
  phase interpolator architecture based on frequency doubling. By operating
  the core interpolation circuitry at half the output frequency, the design
  relaxes speed requirements on the interpolating cells while maintaining
  fine phase resolution at mm-wave frequencies.
\end{abstract}
